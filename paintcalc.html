Privacy Policy

App: Paint Calc (com.balu.paintcalc) · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Paint Calc app (“the app”) handles information. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and does not use advertising or analytics services.

2. Data stored on your device

The values you enter (room sizes, doors and windows, number of coats, coverage, reserve, can sizes) and the projects you choose to save are stored only on your device. We cannot see this data. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device.

3. Internet access and permissions

The app works fully offline. It does not request internet access and does not request any special permissions.

4. Sharing

The “Share” feature creates a plain-text list and passes it to the sharing screen of your Android device. You choose where it goes (for example, a messaging app). We do not receive this content.

5. Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and we do not sell or share information with third parties.

6. Backups

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up app data to your Google account as part of its standard backup feature. This is controlled by your device and Google, not by us. Please see Google’s privacy policy for details.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Estimates disclaimer

The app provides estimates of paint quantity. Actual consumption depends on the surface, paint and application method.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes (for example, if advertising or purchases are added), we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: baluapps.support@gmail.com
