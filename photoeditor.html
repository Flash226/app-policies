Privacy Policy

App: Photo Editor (com.balu.photoeditor) · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Photo Editor app (“the app”) handles information. The short version: your photos stay on your device. The app does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address or location, and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services.

2. Your photos

  • You choose photos with the Android photo picker. The app can only see the photos you select, not your whole gallery.
  • When you take a picture, the app opens your device’s camera app. It does not ask for camera permission itself.
  • The app keeps a private copy of each photo you edit, together with your edits, templates, imported fonts and images, on your device only. Your original photo is never changed.
  • All editing, including background removal and automatic enhancement, runs on your device. Your photos are not sent to us or to any server.

3. Saving and sharing

When you save, the edited image is written to your device (Pictures/PhotoEditor, or Download/PhotoEditor for PDF). When you share, the app passes the image to the sharing screen of your Android device and you choose where it goes. We do not receive this content.

By default, the app removes location data (GPS) from the photos it saves and shares. You can change this in Settings.

4. Internet access and permissions

The app works fully offline and does not have permission to access the internet. On Android 8 and 9 only, it asks for storage permission to save images into your Pictures folder.

5. Google Play services (background removal)

For background removal the app may use Google’s on-device ML Kit subject segmentation, provided by Google Play services. The image is processed on your device. Google Play services may download the required model and operates under Google’s own terms and Privacy Policy. If it is not available, the app uses a model built into the app instead.

6. Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and we do not sell or share information with third parties.

7. Backups

Your photo projects are excluded from Android’s cloud backup and device transfer. Small settings (such as your language or save options) may be included in your device’s standard backup, which is controlled by your device and Google, not by us.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete any project in the app (the original in your gallery stays). Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes (for example, if advertising or purchases are added), we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: baluapps.support@gmail.com
