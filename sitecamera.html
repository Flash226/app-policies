Privacy Policy

App: Site Camera (com.balu.sitecamera) · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Site Camera app (“the app”) handles information. Site Camera is a camera that stamps photos with time, location, altitude, heading, address and notes. The short version: the app has no accounts and no server of its own. Your photos, projects and notes stay on your device. Only the coordinates needed for the address, weather and maps are sent to the services listed below.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address. We, the developer, do not receive your photos, locations, notes or any other content.

2. Your photos, videos and projects

  • Photos and videos you take, the untouched originals, stamps, notes, projects, custom fields and blur areas are stored on your device.
  • Stamped copies are saved to your phone’s gallery (album “Site Camera”). The original is kept inside the app so the stamp can be changed later.
  • When you import photos, you choose them with the Android file or photo picker. The app reads the time and GPS position from the photos you choose, on your device.
  • Face and number-plate detection for privacy blur, QR/barcode scanning and text recognition run on your device using Google ML Kit. The app does not recognize or identify people and does not create, store or share face data.

3. Location

The app uses your location only while it is open (it never requests background location) to put coordinates, altitude and heading on the stamp. The app also works without location permission. Location is stored only with your photos on your device.

4. Data sent over the internet

  • Address — the photo’s coordinates are sent to your device’s geocoding service (Google Play services) to look up the street address.
  • Weather and terrain elevation — only if you turn these on, the coordinates and time of a photo are sent to Open-Meteo.
  • Maps — when you open a map, map tiles for the area you view are downloaded from the map provider (MapTiler, via MapLibre).
  • Google Drive — only if you turn on report upload and grant access, the reports you create are uploaded to your own Google Drive. The app can only access files it created there.
  • Purchases — Site Camera Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only checks whether Pro is active.
  • Diagnostics — the Google ML Kit libraries used for on-device detection may send anonymous usage and performance metrics to Google. No photos are sent.

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos.
  • Location (while the app is in use) — for coordinates on the stamp.
  • Microphone (asked when first needed) — for video sound and voice notes. Speech is recognized by your device’s speech service; the app receives only the text.
  • Photo location — to read GPS data from photos you choose to import.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only) — to save photos to your gallery.

6. Saving and sharing

When you share a photo or report, the app passes it to the sharing screen of your Android device and you choose where it goes. You can choose to remove GPS data from shared files. Privacy blur hides faces and number plates on the stamped copy; the original inside the app is not changed.

7. Advertising

The app currently shows no ads. If ads are added to the free version in the future, they will never appear on the camera screen, this policy will be updated before the change, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it. Pro removes ads.

8. Backups

Photos and videos are excluded from Android’s cloud backup. Project data, notes and settings may be included in your device’s standard backup, which is controlled by your device and Google, not by us.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete any photo or project in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Copies you saved to the gallery, shared or uploaded to your Google Drive stay where you put them.

10. Children’s privacy

The app is a professional tool, is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.

12. Contact
